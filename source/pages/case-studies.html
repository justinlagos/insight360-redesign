Case study 01
Kunoch Foods
Sector
FMCG distribution
Modules
Structure, Data, growth strategy

A stronger operating base for a growing distributor.

The challenge

As Kunoch Foods grew, processes that once worked informally became hard to manage. The business needed better systems behind its daily operations to keep growing.

Our approach

We assessed how the business ran, identified the main operational opportunities and built solutions around its growth plans, focusing on daily operations, the flow of information and strategic decisions.

Areas of focus

  • Operations
  • Process improvement
  • Business systems
  • Data
  • Growth strategy
